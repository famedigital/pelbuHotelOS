Terms & conditions · House policies

By confirming a reservation (email, WhatsApp, allotment, or platform), the travel partner accepts these terms on behalf of the guest(s). MOU partners may hold additional privileges as set out in their signed agreement.

1. Rates & validity

  • Rates on page 1 are in BTN, per room / per night, and include applicable taxes & service charge unless stated otherwise in writing.
  • Quoted rates are for double occupancy. Single occupancy, triple, extra bed, and child rates on request.
  • Valid for Season 2, 2026 for registered travel partners. Subject to change with notice; already confirmed bookings keep the agreed rate.
  • Rates are confidential — not for public websites or guest-facing leaflets without hotel approval.

2. Meal plans

  • EP — room only.
  • CP — room + breakfast for two adults.
  • MAP — room + breakfast + dinner for two adults.
  • Meals are for registered in-house guests unless a day package is booked separately.
  • Special diets: notify the kitchen at least 24 hours before arrival.

3. Confirmation & payment

  • A booking is confirmed only after Pelbu Suites issues written acknowledgment (email / system Voucher).
  • Unless credit / agent ledger terms apply: deposit or full prepayment as requested at confirmation.
  • Agent-credit partners settle per approved credit terms. Overdue accounts may pause new bookings.
  • Accepted: cash (BTN), bank transfer, and approved digital / card methods at desk.
  • Invoices are in BTN. Bank charges, if any, are borne by the payer.

4. Cancellation & no-show

  • Standard season — free cancellation up to 7 full days before the check-in date (local Bhutan time).
  • Peak season — free cancellation up to 14 full days before check-in. Peak windows include (and are not limited to) major tshechus, festivals, high tourist blocks, and dates the hotel publishes as peak; if in doubt, ask at confirmation.
  • Notices must be received in writing (email / WhatsApp) within business hours. The earlier peak window always applies when any night of the stay falls in a peak period.
  • Late cancellation (after the free-cancel window opens): hotel may forfeit deposit and/or charge up to the full reserved stay for the affected room(s).
  • No-show: charge equal to 1 night minimum (room + confirmed meal plan) per reserved room; peak / group holds may be charged for the full stay as stated on the voucher.
  • MOU partners: longer free-cancel windows or fee waivers apply only if written in the MOU.
  • Group blocks / allotments: cancellation may be stricter (e.g. 21–30 days) — as stated on the group contract.

5. Check-in & check-out

  • Check-in from 14:00. Early arrival is subject to room readiness; may carry a charge if confirmed early occupancy.
  • Check-out by 12:00 noon. Late check-out only with prior desk approval; fees may apply.
  • Valid government-issued ID / passport of each adult guest is required at arrival.
  • International tourists: guide details must be provided where required by law or hotel policy.

6. Occupancy & categories

  • Double — 12 rooms · 1 queen · standard double occupancy.
  • Twin — 14 rooms · 2 singles · double occupancy.
  • Suite — 1 room only · private jacuzzi (Suite exclusive; not offered with Double / Twin).
  • Room category is confirmed on allotment; upgrades subject to availability and rate difference.
  • Maximum occupancy per fire / safety capacity — advise party size at booking.

7. Group FOC (10 + 1)

  • For every 10 paying rooms on the same stay dates, the 11th room may be FOC (free of charge).
  • FOC category and meal plan must match an agreed paying room or as confirmed by the hotel in writing.
  • FOC does not automatically stack across split dates or mixed unconfirmed categories.
  • No-show or late cancel of paying rooms may reduce or cancel FOC entitlement.

8. Partner & guest conduct

  • Partners must share accurate guest names, arrival times, meal plan, and special needs before arrival.
  • Damage, loss of keys, or excessive cleaning may be charged to the guest / agent folio.
  • Quiet hours and house rules apply; the hotel may refuse service for misconduct or illegal activity.
  • Smoking only in designated outdoor areas (if any). In-room smoking may attract a cleaning fee.
  • Pets: only with prior written approval.

9. Liability, force majeure & general

  • The hotel is not liable for guest valuables left unsecured; use of in-room storage / desk safe advice is recommended.
  • Service may be limited by circumstances beyond reasonable control (road blocks, power, weather, government order, epidemic). In such cases the hotel will assist with rebooking or fair alternatives where possible without guaranteeing compensation beyond local law.
  • These terms, the confirmed voucher, and any MOU govern the booking. If they conflict, the MOU and written voucher take precedence for that partner.
  • Disputes: amicable resolution first; otherwise subject to the laws of Bhutan and competent courts in Thimphu, unless the MOU states otherwise.
  • Hotel may update published partner tariffs for future seasons; ongoing confirmed stays are not retroactively repriced except by mutual written agreement.
