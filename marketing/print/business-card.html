Print-house brief

  • Trim size: 90 mm × 50 mm (standard Asian business card). Alternative: 85 × 55 mm — scale the PDF 94% / ask the press to centre-crop.
  • Bleed: add 3 mm all around when imposing (artwork here is trim-safe with ~3 mm inner frame).
  • Stock: 350–400 gsm ivory / soft-touch matte, or cotton white. Spot UV optional on gold type / QR quiet zone clear of varnish heavy build-up.
  • Colour: RGB brand file — convert to CMYK on press. Gold ≈ C10 M30 Y80 K5 (approx); ink/espresso ≈ rich black + warm brown.
  • QR: qr-vcard.png encodes a filled vCard — Pelbu Suites, tel +975 1619 3410, email pelbusuites@gmail.com, URL https://www.pelbusuites.bt, address Olakha. Keep quiet zone; do not shrink below ~14 mm square. Rescan after any regenerate.
  • PDF: Chrome → Print → Save as PDF → more settings → paper size custom 90×50 mm · background graphics ON · one side per page.
