Stay terms & house policies

By confirming a reservation (website, WhatsApp, phone, or walk-in deposit), you accept these terms. Live availability and final confirmation always take priority for the booked dates.

1. Rates & validity

  • Package rates on page 1 are in BTN, per room / per night, and are all-in (AI): they already include 10% service charge and 5% GST.
  • Peak and Off season tables apply as tabled. Season dates follow the hotel calendar confirmed at booking.
  • Deluxe King/Twin is one tariff (king or twin bed) — single and double. Pelbu Suite is single & double as tabled.
  • Confirmed bookings keep the agreed rate. Travel agents: use the agent rate card or pelbusuites.bt/agents.

2. Meal plans

  • EPAI — room only (all-in).
  • CPAI — room + breakfast for the priced occupancy.
  • MAPAI — room + breakfast + dinner for the priced occupancy.
  • APAI — room + breakfast, lunch & dinner for the priced occupancy.
  • Special diets: notify the kitchen at least 24 hours before arrival.

3. Confirmation & payment

  • A booking is confirmed only after Pelbu Suites issues written acknowledgment (email / system voucher).
  • Deposit or full prepayment may be requested at confirmation for peak dates.
  • Accepted: cash (BTN), bank transfer, and approved digital / card methods at desk.
  • Invoices are in BTN. Bank charges, if any, are borne by the payer.

4. Cancellation & no-show

  • Standard season — free cancellation up to 7 full days before the check-in date (local Bhutan time).
  • Peak season — free cancellation up to 14 full days before check-in (tshechus, festivals, high tourist blocks, and hotel-declared peak).
  • Notices must be received in writing (email / WhatsApp). The earlier peak window applies when any night is peak.
  • Late cancellation: hotel may forfeit deposit and/or charge up to the full reserved stay.
  • No-show: charge equal to 1 night minimum (room + meal plan) per reserved room; peak as per voucher.

5. Check-in & check-out

  • Check-in from 14:00. Early arrival subject to readiness; fees may apply if confirmed early.
  • Check-out by 12:00 noon. Late check-out only with prior desk approval.
  • Valid government-issued ID / passport for each adult at arrival.
  • International guests: guide details where required by law or hotel policy.

6. Occupancy & categories

  • Deluxe King/Twin — king or twin bed · one tariff · single & double rates as tabled on page 1.
  • Pelbu Suite — 1 room only · private jacuzzi · single & double as priced. Jacuzzi is exclusive to the Suite.
  • Extra bed rates (adult / age 6–12) by plan are on page 1. Ages 0–5 free on child package rules.
  • Category confirmed at booking; upgrades subject to availability and rate difference.

7. Dining & facilities

  • Restaurant, cafe, spa & steam subject to posted hours.
  • Day dining / lunch packages for non-staying guests — schedule in advance when possible.
  • Jacuzzi is exclusive to the Suite room only.

8. Guest conduct

  • Damage, key loss, or excess cleaning may be charged to the folio.
  • Quiet hours and house rules apply; service may be refused for misconduct.
  • Smoking only in designated outdoor areas (if any). Pets only with prior written approval.

9. Liability & general

  • Hotel is not liable for valuables left unsecured. Force majeure may limit service; the hotel will assist reasonably without guaranteeing compensation beyond local law.
  • These terms and the confirmed voucher govern the stay. Questions: WhatsApp +975 1619 3410 · pelbusuites@gmail.com · www.pelbusuites.bt
