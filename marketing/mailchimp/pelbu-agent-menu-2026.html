The café counter

Meet Priya — our barista.

Before Olakha, Priya spent three years as a barista in Kuwait — learning speed, consistency and the craft of a proper cup under real café pressure. Now she pulls espresso, pour-overs and iced classics for your guests who want something better than hotel “coffee machine” fuel before the next day of touring.

Pair her counter with chef-led bites, momos, bakery and café plates — ideal for guides, early check-outs, and free-time mornings between sites.
