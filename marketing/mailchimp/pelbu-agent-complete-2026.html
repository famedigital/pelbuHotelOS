The people behind the plate

A kitchen worth booking for.

Not a franchise kitchen. A compact brigade in Olakha — chefs who know Bhutanese tables, Indian tandoor heat, and the pace of tour groups that arrive hungry after a long day on the road.

Head of kitchen

Chef Jigme Chaeda

Leads the house kitchen with quiet authority — balancing local favourites, Asia plates and the daily rhythm of MAP service so your groups eat well without waiting on the schedule.

Indian cuisine

Chef Pinku

Our specialist for Indian cuisine — tandoor, slow masalas, biryanis and the comfort dishes Indian and regional guests ask for by name. Real depth, not a token “curry section.”

Sous chef

Chef Kelden

Sous chef on the line every service — holding consistency, timing and quality when rooms check in late, when groups order MAP, and when the dining room fills at once.

Kitchen & service

Three additional kitchen colleagues stand with Jigme, Pinku and Kelden — prep, pass and hot station support that keeps a boutique hotel kitchen sharp. On the floor, our F&B team hosts your guests with the same care we ask of the plate.

The café counter

Meet Priya — our barista.

Before Olakha, Priya spent three years as a barista in Kuwait — learning speed, consistency and the craft of a proper cup under real café pressure. Now she pulls espresso, pour-overs and iced classics for your guests who want something better than hotel “coffee machine” fuel before the next day of touring.

Pair her counter with chef-led bites, momos, bakery and café plates — ideal for guides, early check-outs, and free-time mornings between sites.
