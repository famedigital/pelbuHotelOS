Partner handbook

Terms & conditions · Policies

By confirming a reservation (email, WhatsApp, allotment, or platform), the travel partner accepts these terms on behalf of the guest(s). MOU partners may hold additional privileges as set out in their signed agreement.

Cancellation window

Standard: free cancel up to 7 full days before check-in.
Peak season: free cancel up to 14 full days before check-in (festivals, tshechus, high tourist blocks & hotel-published peak dates).
If any night of the stay is peak, the 14-day rule applies. Groups may require 21–30 days as stated on the allotment.

1. Rates & validity

Rates are in BTN, per room / per night, inclusive of applicable taxes & service charge unless written otherwise. Quoted for double occupancy. Valid Season 2, 2026 for registered partners. Confirmed bookings keep the agreed rate. Partner rates are confidential.

2. Meal plans

EP — room only.   CP — room + breakfast for two.   MAP — room + breakfast + dinner for two. Meals for registered in-house guests unless a day package is booked. Special diets: notify us at least 24 hours before arrival.

3. Confirmation & payment

A booking is confirmed only after Pelbu Suites issues written acknowledgment. Deposit or full prepayment as requested, unless agent credit / ledger terms apply. Overdue credit may pause new bookings. Invoices in BTN; bank charges borne by the payer.

4. Cancellation & no-show

See cancellation window above. Late cancel may forfeit deposit and/or charge up to the full reserved stay. No-show: minimum 1 night (room + meal plan) per room; peak / groups may be charged for the full stay as stated on the voucher. MOU waivers only if written in the MOU. Cancel notices must be in writing (email / WhatsApp).

5. Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 14:00. Early arrival subject to readiness (fee may apply). Check-out by 12:00 noon; late check-out only with prior approval. Valid government ID / passport required for each adult. International tourists: guide details where required by law or hotel policy.

6. Occupancy & categories

Double — 12 rooms · 1 queen.   Twin — 14 rooms · 2 singles.   Suite — 1 room only · private jacuzzi (Suite exclusive). Category upgrades subject to availability and rate difference. Advise party size at booking.

7. Group FOC (10 + 1)

For every 10 paying rooms on the same stay dates, the 11th room may be FOC. Category and meal plan as confirmed in writing. FOC does not stack across split dates. Late cancel or no-show of paying rooms may reduce or cancel FOC entitlement.

8. Partner & guest conduct

Share accurate guest names, arrival times, meal plan, and special needs before arrival. Damage, lost keys, or excessive cleaning may be charged to the guest / agent folio. Quiet hours & house rules apply. Smoking only in designated outdoor areas; in-room smoking may attract a cleaning fee. Pets only with prior written approval.

9. Liability, force majeure & general

Hotel is not liable for valuables left unsecured. Service may be limited by circumstances beyond reasonable control; we will assist with fair alternatives where possible. Confirmed voucher + MOU take precedence if they conflict with these terms. Disputes: amicable resolution first; otherwise laws of Bhutan / courts in Thimphu, unless the MOU states otherwise. Future tariff updates do not retroactively reprice confirmed stays.

Acceptance. Confirming a booking constitutes acceptance of these terms and Season 2 partner rates. Free cancel: 7 days standard · 14 days peak.
