THE FUNDAMENTAL TRUTH: ALL BOOKINGS ARE GENERATED BY THE OWNER

Every single tour group contract (Druk Asia, Yangphel, Bhutan Travel Club), corporate group, FIT tourist, and Ngultrum that enters Pelbu Suites is personally brought in through the relationships, negotiations, and financial risk of the OWNER (Rajiv). Bookings do not generate themselves. Staff do not bring in business; staff are hired to PROTECT AND DELIVER the service that keeps those contracts alive. When kitchen cooks drink or abandon lunch, when reception bickers, or when rooms are dirty, you are directly destroying the contracts the Owner fought to bring into this property.

THE GOVERNANCE RULE: NO OWNER OR GM DIRECT INTERFERENCE

The GM is off-island in the Philippines. The Owners (Rajiv & Director of Ops) are the Executive Board. From today, no staff member may approach the Owners' personal residence or office for shift complaints or daily operational disputes. All authority, shift rosters, task assignments, and discipline flow 100% through the appointed on-site Operations Lead.

1. Operational Hierarchy (The Chain of Command)
  • Appointed Operations Lead (HOD): Runs the property ground. Inspects kitchen sobriety, resolves guest/agent complaints, enforces shift rosters, and oversees cashier till cash balances.
  • Front Office (5 Staff): Flight check-ins, guest relations, room allocation, and delivering next-day breakfast counts.
  • Kitchen Team (3 Staff): 06:00 breakfast boxes, 07:00 breakfast buffet, 12:00–14:30 lunch, and 19:00 dinner. Zero alcohol.
  • Housekeeping (6 Staff): Pristine room turnaround, linen wash, corridor hygiene, and evening rollaway deployment.
  • F&B Service (3 Staff): Courteous table service, buffet warmth, bar monitoring, and instant KOT billing.
  • Reservations (1 Staff): Strictly manages agency contracts, allotments, rate quotes, and Hotel OS holds. Does not manage field operations.
2. Mandatory Daily Management Rhythm

Finance & Payroll Policy · Executive Order #02

SALARY ADVANCES & S.C. CHARTER Cash Flow Discipline & Fairness
FINANCE & PAYROLL CONTROLLER:
Applicable to: All 19 Staff Members
STRICT POLICY: THE BAN ON NON-STOP SALARY ADVANCES

Staff members (specifically in Kitchen, F&B, and Front Office) have been taking non-stop cash advances, depleting the hotel till and leaving zero salary at month-end. Housekeeping and Reservations are hereby officially commended for their financial discipline in NOT abusing advances. From today, casual borrowing from the front desk or owner is permanently terminated.

1. The New Salary Advance Rules (Strictly Enforced)
Rule Criterion Mandatory Standard & Limitation
Eligible Date Advances are permitted ONLY AFTER the 15th of the month. No advance will be disbursed during the first 14 days of any month.
Maximum Ceiling Maximum 30% of base monthly salary. You cannot borrow money you have not yet worked to earn.
Frequency Cap Maximum 1 advance per calendar month per employee. Zero exceptions.
Clear Balance Rule Any prior advance balance must be 100% cleared before a new request can be considered.
Approval Process Written slip submitted to Operations Lead → Forwarded to Director of Operations for approval → Disbursed via bank transfer. Never taken directly from the Front Desk Cashier Till.
Disciplinary Ban Any employee with an active disciplinary strike or alcohol violation is strictly barred from receiving salary advances.
2. Service Charge (S.C.) Reality: Peak Season vs. 3-Month Off-Season

Service Charge (10%) is an earned statutory pool collected directly from paying hotel guests. It is NOT a guaranteed fixed salary entitlement.

  • Peak Season (Spring & Autumn): When tour groups and festival guests fill the hotel, the 10% S.C. pool is large and distributed transparently among eligible staff based on attendance and performance.
  • The 3-Month Off-Season Reality (Winter Dec–Feb / Monsoon): During these 3 months, Bhutan experiences extreme low season. Pelbu Suites hardly makes any sales. When guest revenue is near zero, the collected Service Charge pool is naturally near zero. Staff cannot expect peak season S.C. payouts when hotel rooms are empty. The Owner guarantees your base monthly salary even when the hotel operates at a loss in off-season.
  • Performance Deductions: Any staff member who arrives smelling of alcohol, abandons the kitchen at lunch, or incurs a verified guest complaint forfeits their entire monthly S.C. share.
3. Cashier Till Vault Protection

The Front Desk Till Vault (Nu. 48,250 baseline) exists solely for guest cash changes, foreign currency exchanges, and official hotel emergency purchases. Taking staff personal advances or personal loans from the Front Desk Till is treated as financial embezzlement.

Staff Code of Conduct & Disciplinary Policy

ENFORCEMENT POLICY Workplace Discipline & Fairness
ZERO-TOLERANCE OFFENSES (IMMEDIATE ACTION)

The following 4 violations result in immediate suspension, forfeiture of monthly Service Charge (SC), and possible termination under the Labour and Employment Act of Bhutan:

  • 1. Alcohol or Narcotics: Consuming alcohol before or during shift, or reporting to duty with breath alcohol.
  • 2. Abandonment of Post: Leaving kitchen, front desk, or dining room unattended during meal or arrival hours.
  • 3. Casual Advance Demands: Demanding daily/weekly cash advances from the Front Desk Till or harassing management for loans.
  • 4. Insubordination & Bypassing: Refusing direct orders from the Operations Lead or bypassing the HOD to complain to the Owners.
1. The 3-Strike Disciplinary Framework
Offense Level Administrative Action Financial & Service Charge Penalty
1st Strike Official Written Warning logged in staff HR file by Operations Lead. Forfeiture of 50% of monthly Service Charge (SC) pool.
2nd Strike Final Written Warning and mandatory hearing with Owners. Deduction of 2 days' base wages + 100% loss of monthly SC + Advance Ban.
3rd Strike Immediate Termination of Employment contract. Formal reporting to Ministry of Industry, Commerce & Employment (MoICE).
2. All-Staff Commitment & Acknowledgment

"I hereby acknowledge that I have received, read, and understood the Standard Operating Procedures, Shift Timings, Salary Advance Rules, and Management Guidelines for Pelbu Suites. I acknowledge that 100% of hotel bookings are brought in by the Owner, and my duty is to deliver flawless hospitality to protect those bookings. I agree to take operational direction from the appointed on-site Operations Lead. I understand that reporting to duty under the influence of alcohol, fighting, demanding unapproved cash advances, or deserting my post will result in immediate wage deductions, forfeiture of Service Charge, and termination."
