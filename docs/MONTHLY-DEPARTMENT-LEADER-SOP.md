# Monthly Department Leader — Hotel SOP

**Property:** Pelbu Suites  
**Starts:** 1 October 2026  
**Pay:** Nu. 4,000 extra for the month a person leads, only if they performed. If they do not perform, the extra is not paid.  
**Reports to:** Management — Rajiv (Owner), Tshering (Ops), Dawa (GM, online), Choki (Reservations)

One person in each working department is **Leader of the Month**. They still do their own job. On top of that, they are the person Management holds responsible for that department: people on time, stores used properly, and guests leaving happy.

Choki does not rotate. Reservations stays with Management.

## 3. What the leader is responsible for

The leader is fully responsible for their department’s service that month. “I told them” is not a result. The result is: staff were on time, the guest got what was promised, and nothing was wasted or taken.

### Timekeeping

- The leader is on duty before the team.
- At the start of each shift, the leader marks who is present, late, or absent, and sends it to Tshering before the shift is 15 minutes old.
- A late person is noted. Repeat lateness in the same week is reported the same day, not saved for month-end.
- A no-show is covered inside the department first. If the shift cannot be covered, Tshering is called before guests are affected.
- The leader does not mark a friend present who is not there. That ends the month early.

### Stores and shopping

The standard is **enough to serve, nothing extra, nothing personal**. Only two people go to market.

| What | Who buys | Who else may touch it |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen and F&B, together | The one named shopper that month | The other leader counts and signs their own lines. They do not buy. |
| Housekeeping | The Housekeeping leader that month | Other HK staff count and tell the leader. They do not buy. |
| Front Office | Nobody. Tshering buys from a short list, or adds it to a shopper’s list | The FO leader writes what the desk is short of |

- Before a list is written, stock is counted. The list names the item, quantity, and why it is needed this week. “We always buy this” is not a reason.
- Kitchen and F&B share **one list**. Food for the kitchen and goods for the restaurant go on that same paper. One trip.
- No personal use: food, amenities, linen, cleaning chemical, or cash do not go home. Staff meals stay on the staff-meal rule already in use.
- Both leaders sign the Kitchen & F&B list (each signs their own lines). The HK leader signs the HK list. Tshering approves and releases cash **only to the named shopper** before anything is bought. Rajiv approves anything that is not a normal weekly item, or any single item above **Nu. 5,000**.
- If the shopper is off on market day, only that person’s backup shops. Another department does not take the cash.
- The shopper comes back the same day with the goods, the bills, and the change. The leader of each department checks their own goods against the list. Short, damaged, or extra items are written on the list and given to Tshering with the bills.
- Lists and bills stay with Tshering and are handed to the next shopper on the last day of the month.

### Service and the guest

Every leader, in their own area, makes sure the person in front of them is looked after.

| Department | The guest should feel |
|---|---|
| Front Office | Expected, checked in without a wait, room matches the booking, checkout is clear and polite |
| Food & Beverage | Greeted, order correct, food and drink on time, table cleared, bill right |
| Kitchen | Food matches the order, hot, on time, and safe. 86 an item early, not after the guest has ordered it |
| Housekeeping | Room ready before arrival, clean, quiet, complete. A defect is fixed before the guest sleeps there |

House rules for every leader:

- Speak to guests calmly and by name when the name is known.
- A complaint is owned by the leader of that department until it is fixed or Tshering has taken it.
- Fix it on the same shift. If it cannot be fixed, tell the guest what will happen and by when, then tell Tshering and Choki.
- Do not argue with a guest. Do not promise a discount, a free night, or a refund. That is Tshering or Rajiv.
- Do not ask for a review while the guest is still unhappy. Fix the stay first.

### Reviews

The aim is a true 5-star review because the stay was good, not because someone begged at the door.

- Choki watches new reviews each day and posts anything below 5 stars in the Management group within the hour of seeing it.
- The leader of the department named in the review replies to Management the same day: what happened, who was on duty, what will change tomorrow.
- At checkout, Front Office invites a Google review only when the guest has said the stay was good.
- A guest who complained is recovered before departure. Recovery is Tshering’s call.

## 4. The leader’s day

**Before the shift**

1. Be there first.
2. Read yesterday’s handover and today’s arrivals (Choki’s list).
3. Walk the area: desk, restaurant, kitchen pass, or rooms due out and due in.
4. Mark attendance and send it.

**During the shift**

5. Put the team on the work. Say the standard out loud when it is missed, the same day, in private.
6. Catch empty stock, a dirty room, a late tray, or a sharp word to a guest while it is still small.
7. Tell the shopper what is short. Do not buy it yourself unless you are the named shopper that month.

**Every day at 10:00 — signed briefing**

All four leaders meet Tshering at **10:00 sharp**, every day the hotel is open. The time does not move to “when we are free.” Choki brings today’s arrivals. Rajiv and Dawa join when they can. Dawa may join online. The meeting is 15 minutes.

The signed sheet is the proof. If it is not signed, the briefing did not happen.

Order, same every day:

1. Tshering opens a fresh sheet and writes the date. Start time is 10:00.
2. Choki reads arrivals, departures, groups, early check-ins, and any promise already made to a guest.
3. Each leader, in this order — Front Office, Food & Beverage, Kitchen, Housekeeping — gives only these four lines:
   - Team: who is in, who is late, who is absent
   - Guest: any complaint or request still open
   - Ready: rooms, meals, or desk work that is not ready for today
   - Stores: anything that will run out before the next approved buy
4. Tshering gives the instruction for the day. It is written on the sheet. It is not debated in the meeting.
5. Each leader signs. Tshering signs last and writes the time the meeting closed.

Rules of the sheet:

- Tshering keeps it. It does not go home with a leader. One sheet per day, filed by month.
- A leader who is out: the backup attends and signs “for [name].” Tshering writes why.
- A leader who is in the building but misses 10:00 reads the sheet and signs before their shift ends the same day. Tshering writes the actual time next to that signature. The miss is noted.
- No signature by the end of that day means that leader was not briefed. It cannot be signed the next morning.
- Three unsigned days in the month fails the briefing check for the Nu. 4,000.
- A day with no sheet at all is a missed meeting. It is not rewritten the next day.

**End of shift**

Five-line handover to the next shift and to Tshering:

- Who was late or absent
- Guest issues still open
- Rooms or meals not ready
- Stock that will run out before the next buy
- Anything Management must decide

## 5. The leader’s week and month

**Weekly — 30 minutes with Management**  
Tshering in the room. Rajiv, Dawa, and Choki join in person or online.

Each leader brings:

- Attendance: days late, days absent, covers used
- Requisitions: what was asked, what was approved, what arrived, what was left unused
- Guest issues: what happened, how it ended
- One thing the department will do better next week

Dawa’s part of this meeting is standards and any review from the week. Choki’s part is upcoming groups, early arrivals, and booking promises the floor must keep.

**Last day of the month — handover, 20 minutes**

Outgoing leader to incoming leader, with Tshering present:

- Open complaints
- Half-done requisitions and where the goods are
- Who is reliable on early shift, who needs watching
- Keys, lists, and any cash or stores the leader was holding

The incoming leader starts the next morning. There is no gap.

**Month score — Management decides the Nu. 4,000**

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Daily 10:00 briefing | Signed the sheet the same day. Three unsigned days fails this check |
| Attendance sent on time | Lists exist for the days they led |
| No false attendance | Spot-check matches the list |
| Shopping | Only the two named shoppers bought. Kitchen and F&B were one trip. Bills and change came back the same day |
| Guest complaints in their area | Same-shift response, logged to Tshering |
| Handover | Next leader can start without guessing |
| Reviews tied to their department | Answered the same day with a fix |

All seven must pass. Then the Nu. 4,000 is paid with that month’s salary. Rajiv can ask to see the signed sheets before it is released.

If any check fails, the extra is not paid. There is no half payment and no “warning instead of the money.” Tshering tells the leader before payday, and the sheets are the proof. Basic salary is not cut by this rule. Misuse, false attendance, or a guest left angry with no report also ends the month early, as in section 1.

## 6. What the leader does not do

- Hire, fire, cut salary, or change days off. Those stay with Rajiv and Tshering.
- Go to market, or hold shopping cash, unless you are that month’s named shopper. Kitchen and F&B are one shopper. Housekeeping is one shopper.
- Spend hotel money without Tshering’s approval.
- Discount a bill, void a charge, or give a room free.
- Discipline another department.
- Speak for the hotel on a public review. Choki drafts, Dawa or Tshering approves.
