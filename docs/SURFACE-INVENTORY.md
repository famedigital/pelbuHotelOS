## Glossary (FO money)

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Folio / bill | Running stay bill (room + extras + payments) |
| Invoice INV-… | Tax invoice issued from a folio |
| PS-… | Stay confirmation — not a tax invoice |
| Statement (Finance) | Hotel books / cash position — **not** a guest bill |
| Who owes | Open balances to collect (guest or agent) |
