Shopping rules

  • Count what is already there before writing a list. Name the item, the quantity, and why it is needed this week.
  • Kitchen and F&B use one list. The leader who is not shopping signs their own lines, then stays at the hotel.
  • Tshering approves the list and gives cash only to the named shopper. Rajiv approves anything unusual, or any single item above Nu. 5,000.
  • If the shopper is off, only their backup goes. Another department does not take the cash.
  • Goods, bills, and change come back the same day. Each leader checks their own goods. Nothing goes home.

What a leader does not do

  • Hire, fire, cut salary, or change days off.
  • Go to market unless they are that month’s named shopper.
  • Discount a bill, void a charge, or give a room free.
  • Promise a refund. That is Tshering or Rajiv.

Staff SOP

10:00 briefing
signed

All four leaders meet Tshering at 10:00 sharp. The time does not move. Choki brings arrivals. Rajiv and Dawa join when they can. Dawa may join online. Fifteen minutes. The signed sheet is the proof. If it is not signed, the briefing did not happen.

Same order every day

  1. Tshering opens a fresh sheet and writes the date.
  2. Choki reads arrivals, departures, groups, early check-ins, and any promise already made to a guest.
  3. Each leader, in order — Front Office, F&B, Kitchen, Housekeeping — gives four lines only: who is in or absent; any guest issue still open; what is not ready today; what will run out before the next buy.
  4. Tshering writes the instruction for the day. It is not debated in the meeting.
  5. Each leader signs. Tshering signs last and writes the time the meeting closed.

The sheet

  • Tshering keeps it. It does not go home. One sheet per day, filed by month.
  • A leader who is out: the backup signs “for [name].” Tshering writes why.
  • A leader who misses 10:00 but is in the building reads the sheet and signs before their shift ends the same day. Tshering writes the time. The miss is noted.
  • No signature by the end of that day cannot be added the next morning.
  • Three unsigned days in the month fails the briefing check for the Nu. 4,000.
  • Rajiv can ask to see the month’s signed sheets before the extra pay is released.

Guest standard

DepartmentThe guest should feel
Front OfficeExpected, checked in without a wait, room matches the booking, checkout clear and polite.

KitchenFood matches the order, hot, on time, and safe. An item that is finished is taken off before the guest orders it.

A complaint is owned by that department’s leader until it is fixed or Tshering has taken it. Do not ask for a review while the guest is still unhappy. Choki posts anything under 5 stars to Management the same day. The leader answers with what happened and what changes tomorrow.

Month score for the Nu. 4,000

The extra is paid only when all of these are true: signed the 10:00 sheet (fewer than three misses); attendance sent and honest; only the two shoppers bought, with bills back the same day; guest complaints handled the same shift; handover done; reviews in their department answered the same day. Miss one and the Nu. 4,000 is not paid. No half payment. Tshering tells them before payday. The signed sheets are the proof.
