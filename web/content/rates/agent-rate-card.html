Agent terms & conditions

By confirming a reservation (email, WhatsApp, allotment, or agent portal), the travel agent accepts these terms on behalf of the guest(s). MOU partners may hold additional privileges as set out in their signed agreement.

1. Rates & validity

  • Rates on page 1 are in BTN, per room / per night, inclusive of applicable taxes & service charge unless written otherwise.
  • The published tariff is a special discounted peak-season rate for stays with nights in September, October, and/or November 2026.
  • Priced occupancy: Deluxe Queen = single and double; Twin = double only (no single); Suite = double. Extra bed on request.
  • Outside peak months, standard season tariffs apply — confirm with the desk. Confirmed bookings keep the agreed rate.
  • Agent rates are confidential — not for public websites or guest leaflets without approval.

2. Meal plans

  • EP — room only.
  • CP — room + breakfast for the priced occupancy (1 adult on Deluxe Queen single; 2 adults on double / Twin / Suite).
  • MAP — room + breakfast + dinner for the priced occupancy.
  • Day packages and special diets: notify kitchen ≥ 24 hours before meal service.

3. Confirmation & payment

  • Confirmed only after Pelbu Suites issues written acknowledgment (email / voucher).
  • Unless agent credit / ledger terms apply: deposit or full prepayment as requested at confirmation.
  • Agent-credit accounts settle per approved terms; overdue may pause new bookings.
  • Accepted: BTN cash, bank transfer, and approved digital / card methods at desk. Bank charges borne by payer.

4. Cancellation & no-show

  • Standard — free cancel up to 7 full days before check-in (Bhutan time).
  • Peak — free cancel up to 14 full days before check-in (festivals, tshechus, high tourist blocks, and hotel-declared peak).
  • Notice in writing (email / WhatsApp). Earlier peak window applies if any night is peak.
  • Late cancel: deposit forfeit and/or charge up to full reserved stay.
  • No-show: min. 1 night (room + meal plan) per room; peak / groups as per voucher.
  • Group blocks may require 21–30 days — as on the group contract.

5. Check-in & check-out

  • Check-in from 14:00. Early arrival subject to readiness; fees may apply.
  • Check-out by 12:00. Late check-out only with prior approval.
  • Valid ID / passport for each adult. International: guide details where required.

6. Occupancy & categories

  • Deluxe Queen — 12 rooms · 1 queen · single & double as priced.
  • Twin — 14 rooms · 2 singles · double occupancy only (no single rate).
  • Suite — 1 only · private jacuzzi · double occupancy (Suite exclusive).
  • Guide & driver beds: complementary on qualifying agent groups — confirm at booking.

7. Group FOC (10 + 1)

  • Every 10 paying rooms on the same stay dates → 11th room may be FOC.
  • FOC category / meal plan as confirmed in writing. Late cancel / no-show of paying rooms may cancel FOC.

8. Agent conduct

  • Share accurate guest names, ETA, meal plan, and special needs before arrival.
  • Damage, key loss, or excess cleaning may be charged to the guest / agent folio.
  • House rules apply; smoking only in designated outdoor areas if any.

9. Liability & general

  • Hotel not liable for unsecured valuables. Force majeure may limit service; hotel will assist reasonably without guaranteeing compensation beyond law.
  • Voucher + MOU (if any) + these terms govern the stay. Disputes: laws of Bhutan / Thimphu courts unless MOU says otherwise.
